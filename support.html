Getting the best result

Use a clear, well-lit, front-facing photo with your cat's face and upper body visible. AI generation requires an internet connection, and results may vary.

↻

Restore Catdress Pro

  1. Open Catdress and tap a locked outfit or the Go Pro banner.
  2. On the subscription screen, tap Restore Purchase.
  3. Make sure the device uses the same Apple Account that made the purchase.
♛

Manage or cancel a subscription

Open Settings on your device, tap your name, choose Subscriptions, then select Catdress. Canceling stops future renewals while preserving access through the current billing period.

⌫

Delete your account

After linking with Sign in with Apple, open Catdress, tap the information icon, and choose Delete Account. Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Still need help?

Tell us your device model, iOS version, Catdress app version, and what happened. Please do not email passwords or payment information.
